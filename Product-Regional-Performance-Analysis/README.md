Project Overview

This project analyzed product performance, retailer contribution, regional sales distribution, and sales method effectiveness using Microsoft Excel. Interactive charts and Pivot Tables were used to identify trends and compare sales performance across different regions and product lines.

The objective was to evaluate business performance and uncover patterns that could support strategic decision-making.

Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Dashboard Visualization
* Trend Analysis

Dataset Description

The dataset included:

* Product categories
* Regional sales records
* Retailer performance data
* Sales methods
* Monthly sales trends

Dashboard Features

1. Sales Trend Analysis

A line chart was used to monitor sales performance over time.

Key Insight

Sales levels experienced fluctuations throughout the year, with noticeable peak and low-performing periods.

2. Retailer Performance Analysis

A horizontal bar chart compared retailer contributions across regions.

Key Insight

Some retailers consistently outperformed others, showing stronger sales presence and customer reach.

3. Sales Method Contribution

A pie chart was used to analyze contributions from different sales channels such as:

* Online
* Outlet
* In-store

Key Insight

One sales channel contributed the largest percentage of total sales revenue, indicating stronger customer preference.

4. Product vs Region Analysis

A clustered chart compared product performance across different regions.

Key Insight

Product demand varied significantly across regions, showing differences in customer preferences and purchasing behavior.

5. Top Performing Product Lines Across Regions

The dashboard identified the highest-performing product lines in different regions.

Key Insight

Certain product lines consistently dominated sales across multiple regions.

Role of Pivot Tables

Pivot Tables were used extensively to:

* Aggregate regional sales data
* Compare product performance
* Calculate sales contributions
* Build dynamic dashboard charts

Business Recommendations

* Increase investment in top-performing sales channels
* Develop region-specific marketing strategies
* Strengthen partnerships with high-performing retailers
* Focus inventory planning around best-selling product lines

Conclusion

This project demonstrates strong Excel analytical skills through the use of:

* Pivot Tables
* Interactive dashboards
* Trend analysis
* Business reporting

The analysis provided actionable insights into sales performance, regional demand, and customer purchasing behavior.
