Business Intelligence Dashboard Projects

Power BI and Tableau Sales Analysis

Project Overview

These projects focused on building interactive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboards using both Power BI and Tableau to analyze sales and profit performance from a Superstore dataset.

The dashboards were designed to transform raw business data into visual insights that support decision-making, performance evaluation, and trend analysis. In addition to creating the dashboards, a detailed analytical report was also prepared to explain findings, observations, and recommendations derived from the visualizations.

The projects demonstrate practical skills in:

* Data visualization
* Business intelligence reporting
* Dashboard development
* Data storytelling
* Analytical reporting


Project 1: Power BI Superstore Dashboard

Overview

This project involved creating an interactive dashboard in  Microsoft Power BI￼ to analyze sales, profit, discounts, regional performance, and product category trends.

The dashboard combined multiple visualizations into a single interactive reporting environment.


Features of the Dashboard

1. Sales by Region

A pie chart visualization showing total sales contribution across different regions.

Purpose

* Compare regional sales performance
* Identify high-performing regions
* Support regional business strategies


2. Profit by Sub-Category

A horizontal bar chart displaying profit generated by different product sub-categories.

Purpose

* Identify the most profitable products
* Detect underperforming categories
* Support inventory and marketing decisions


3. Sales, Discount, and Profit Analysis

A scatter plot visualization analyzing the relationship between discounts, sales, and profit.

Purpose

* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability
* Identify risky discount patterns
* Support pricing strategy decisions


4. Monthly Sales and Profit Trend

A line chart showing monthly trends in sales and profit.

Purpose

* Observe seasonal sales patterns
* Track business growth over time
* Identify peak and low-performing months


5. Geographic Sales Distribution

A map visualization displaying sales distribution across states and categories.

Purpose

* Analyze geographical sales patterns
* Identify strong and weak market locations
* Support regional expansion strategies


KPIs Included

The dashboard also displayed important business metrics such as:

* Total Profit
* Total Sales

These KPIs provided quick performance summaries for decision-makers.


Project 2: Tableau Superstore Dashboard

Overview

This project involved creating an interactive dashboard using  Tableau￼ for sales and profit analysis using the same Superstore dataset.

The Tableau dashboard focused on presenting business insights through advanced visual storytelling and interactive filtering.


Features of the Dashboard

1. Sales by Region Visualization

Bubble chart visualization comparing sales contributions across regions.

Purpose

* Provide visual comparison of regional sales
* Highlight dominant sales regions


2. Monthly Profit and Sales Trend

A line graph showing monthly changes in sales and profit.

Purpose

* Identify sales growth patterns
* Analyze business performance trends over time


3. Sales by State and Category

A geographical map displaying sales activity by state and product category.

Purpose

* Understand geographic market distribution
* Compare category performance across locations


4. Profit by Category/Sub-Category

A category comparison chart showing profits generated by product groups.

Purpose

* Identify high-performing product segments
* Detect categories with losses or low profitability


5. Discount and Profit Relationship

A visualization examining the relationship between discounts and profits.

Purpose

* Analyze the effect of discounts on business returns
* Support pricing and promotional strategies
