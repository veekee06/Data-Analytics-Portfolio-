Adidas Sales Dashboard Analysis Project

Project Overview

This project is a Sales Dashboard Analysis created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard was developed as a group project to analyze sales performance across different regions, product categories, retailers, and sales methods.

The project made extensive use of Pivot Tables to summarize large datasets and transform raw sales data into meaningful visual insights for decision-making.

In addition to data visualization and analysis, the team also provided business recommendations based on the insights generated from the Pivot Tables and dashboards.

Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze sales trends over time
* Compare retailer performance
* Evaluate sales contribution by sales methods
* Examine regional product performance
* Present business insights using interactive dashboards
* Develop practical data analysis and visualization skills using Excel
* Make data-driven recommendations for improved business performance

Dashboard Components

1. Sales Trend Analysis

This dashboard visualizes sales performance across different periods and compares yearly trends.

Insights Generated

* Identification of peak sales periods
* Comparison of yearly sales growth
* Observation of fluctuations in sales performance

Recommendations

* Increase inventory during peak sales periods
* Introduce promotional campaigns during low-performing months
* Focus marketing efforts on consistently high-performing periods

2. Retailer’s Performance

This section evaluates the performance of different retailers based on total sales and quantity sold.

Metrics Analyzed

* Total Sales
* Quantity of Units Sold
* Percentage Contribution

Recommendations

* Strengthen partnerships with top-performing retailers
* Provide incentives to underperforming retailers
* Allocate more resources to high-revenue sales channels

3. Sales Method Contribution

A pie chart dashboard showing the contribution of different sales channels or methods.

Metrics Analyzed

* In-store Sales
* Online Sales
* Outlet Sales

Recommendations

* Invest more in the highest-performing sales channel
* Improve digital marketing strategies for online sales
* Enhance customer experience across weaker channels

4. Product vs Region Analysis

This dashboard compares product performance across various geographical regions.

Regions Included

* West
* Northeast
* Southeast
* South
* Midwest

Recommendations

* Focus distribution efforts on regions with high demand
* Develop targeted regional marketing strategies
* Adjust product supply according to regional demand patterns

5. Top Performing Product Line Across Regions

This section highlights the best-selling product categories across multiple regions using comparative charts.

Recommendations

* Increase production of top-performing products
* Promote weaker product lines strategically
* Use regional preferences to guide future product planning

Role of Pivot Tables

Pivot Tables were the core analytical tool used in this project.

They were used to:

* Summarize large datasets efficiently
* Calculate totals and percentages
* Group data by region, retailer, product, and year
* Generate dynamic charts for the dashboard
* Improve data interpretation and reporting
* Support data-driven recommendations and business decisions

The project helped improve teamwork, analytical thinking, communication, and practical Excel reporting skills.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Pivot Tables and dashboards in Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into interactive visual reports. The dashboard provides valuable insights into sales trends, retailer performance, product demand, and regional contributions.

Beyond visualization, the project also emphasized the importance of interpreting data and making informed recommendations that can support business growth, operational efficiency, and strategic decision-making.
