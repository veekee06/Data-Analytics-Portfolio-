Hospital Patient Analysis Dashboard

This project is a Hospital Patient Analysis Dashboard developed as part of a group project during my data analytics course. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, billing performance, insurance coverage, and operational efficiency within healthcare facilities.

The goal of the project was to analyze healthcare data and generate actionable insights that can support better decision-making in hospitals and healthcare management.


Project Overview

The dashboard analyzes approximately:

* 40.2K Total Patients
* 56K Total Admissions
* 1.42bn Total Billing Amount
* 25.54K Average Billing Amount

The project focuses on identifying healthcare trends, improving operational efficiency, and supporting strategic healthcare planning through interactive data visualization.


Key Areas of Analysis

Patient Care Optimization

The dashboard highlights major medical conditions affecting patients, including:

* Arthritis
* Diabetes
* Hypertension
* Obesity
* Cancer
* Asthma

Key findings showed that older adults, particularly patients aged 56 years and above, recorded the highest number of medical condition cases. The analysis also revealed a significant number of abnormal and inconclusive test results, emphasizing the need for improved diagnostics and preventive healthcare programs.


Hospital Resource Allocation

The project examined hospital workload distribution and patient volume trends.

Insights revealed that:

* Some hospitals and doctors handled significantly higher patient volumes than others
* Admission rates remained consistently high between 2020 and 2023
* Certain hospitals generated higher billing amounts due to increased patient traffic and treatment complexity

Recommendations focused on:

* Better staff allocation
* Improved referral systems
* Enhanced workforce planning
* Resource optimization across healthcare facilities


Insurance & Billing Strategy

The dashboard analyzed the relationship between:

* Insurance providers
* Billing amounts
* Admission types
* Revenue generation

Major insurance providers included:

* Cigna
* Medicare
* Blue Cross
* UnitedHealthcare
* Aetna

The analysis showed that hospitals with higher patient volumes also generated higher billing revenue, emphasizing the importance of financial planning and strategic partnerships with insurance providers.


Preventive Health Focus

The analysis identified chronic diseases and older age groups as major healthcare risk areas.

The project recommended:

* Community health awareness campaigns
* Preventive screening programs
* Wellness initiatives
* Early intervention strategies for high-risk demographics


Operational Efficiency

The dashboard evaluated:

* Admission trends over time
* Emergency vs elective admissions
* Patient flow management
* Hospital operational readiness

The analysis showed relatively balanced admission types across:

* Elective
* Urgent
* Emergency admissions

This indicated the need for hospitals to maintain consistent operational preparedness across all admission categories.
